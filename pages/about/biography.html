About Me

Vijay Kumar Chauhan is a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Sustainable Energy Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur under the supervision of Prof. Jishnu Bhattacharya and Prof. Vaibhav Arghode.

His research focuses on lithium-ion battery characterization, battery thermal management, electrochemical modelling, equivalent circuit modelling, calorimetric measurements, battery safety, fast charging, battery ageing, digital twins, and artificial intelligence for electric vehicle and energy storage applications.

His work combines advanced experimental investigations using Isothermal Battery Calorimetry (IBC) and Adiabatic Rate Calorimetry (ARC) with electrochemical modelling, numerical simulations and machine learning techniques to accurately predict battery heat generation, improve safety, and optimize Battery Thermal Management Systems (BTMS).

His long-term research vision is to develop intelligent Battery Management Systems, Digital Twin frameworks and physics-informed AI models that enable safer, smarter and high-performance lithium-ion batteries for future electric mobility and renewable energy systems.
