Research Interests

Advancing lithium-ion battery technologies through experimental characterization, numerical modelling, battery safety and artificial intelligence.

🔬

Experimental Research

Developing advanced experimental methodologies to understand electrochemical and thermal behaviour of lithium-ion batteries.

  • Battery Characterization
  • Experimental Calorimetry
  • Heat Generation Measurement
  • Performance Evaluation
  • Battery Testing
⚙️

Physics-Based Battery Modeling

Developing electrochemical and equivalent circuit models for accurate prediction of battery behaviour.

  • Pseudo Two-Dimensional (P2D)
  • Equivalent Circuit Modeling (ECM)
  • Heat Generation Modeling
  • Electro-Thermal Simulation
  • Digital Twin Development
🤖

Artificial Intelligence

Applying machine learning and deep learning techniques for battery diagnostics and thermal prediction.

  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Symbolic Regression
  • SHAP Analysis
  • Predictive Analytics
🛡

Battery Safety & Reliability

Enhancing battery safety through thermal management, aging studies and abuse testing.

  • Battery Aging
  • Fast Charging
  • Thermal Runaway
  • Battery Safety
  • Abuse Testing

Current & Future Research

My current research focuses on the development of advanced Battery Thermal Management Systems for battery packs, thermal runaway mitigation, abuse testing of cylindrical, pouch and prismatic lithium-ion batteries, digital twin development, and AI-enabled battery management systems for electric vehicle applications.
