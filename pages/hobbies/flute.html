MY MUSICAL JOURNEY

Learning Through Practice

Learning the flute has become a personal journey of patience, practice and continuous exploration. I started with the C-middle-scale flute and gradually developed my foundation through regular practice.

Much of my learning has been self-guided. I have learned by observing performances and instructional videos from accomplished flautists and then incorporating those ideas into my own practice.

Over time, my practice has progressed from basic alankars and note exercises to long-note practice and Indian classical ornamentation techniques such as meend, gamak, murki, kan swar, khaka and vibrato.

Progress comes from consistent practice, one note at a time.

INDIAN CLASSICAL TECHNIQUES

Exploring Ornamentation

I am gradually learning different ornamentation techniques to add expression, movement and character to notes and ragas.

01

Meend

Smooth movement from one note to another.

02

Gamak

Powerful oscillation used for musical expression.

03

Murki

Quick and delicate movement between notes.

04

Kan Swar

Grace-note treatment that adds subtle expression.

05

Khaka

A pattern-based approach to melodic expression.

06

Vibrato

Controlled variation in pitch for expressive playing.
