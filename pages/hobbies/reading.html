PERSONAL INTEREST

Reading & Reflections

Books that have shaped the way I think, learn, work and understand life.

MY READING LIST

Books I've Read

A small collection of books that offered valuable perspectives on life, habits, money, mindset and purpose.

01
PHILOSOPHY LIFE

Bhagavad Gita

A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada

In brief

A timeless dialogue on duty, action, self-discipline and maintaining balance while facing uncertainty and challenges.

MY TAKEAWAY

Focus on the quality of your actions rather than being consumed by the outcome.

02
HABITS SELF-DEVELOPMENT

Atomic Habits

James Clear

In brief

A practical approach to building better habits through small, consistent improvements and intentional changes to everyday behaviour.

MY TAKEAWAY

Small improvements, repeated consistently, can create remarkable long-term results.

03
FINANCE BEHAVIOUR

The Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel

In brief

Explores how behaviour, emotions and personal experiences influence financial decisions more than financial knowledge alone.

MY TAKEAWAY

Financial success is often more about behaviour and discipline than technical knowledge.

04
MINDSET PERSONAL GROWTH

The Power of Your Subconscious Mind

Joseph Murphy

In brief

Explores the influence of subconscious thought patterns and beliefs on behaviour, mindset and personal development.

MY TAKEAWAY

The way we repeatedly think can strongly influence the way we act and respond.

05
PURPOSE LIFE

Ikigai

Héctor García & Francesc Miralles

In brief

Introduces the idea of finding meaning and fulfilment by connecting purpose, interests, abilities and everyday life.

MY TAKEAWAY

A meaningful life can emerge from finding purpose in what we do every day.

06
HINDI LITERATURE FICTION

Gunahon Ka Devta

Dharamvir Bharati

In brief

A celebrated Hindi literary novel exploring love, relationships, emotions and the difficult choices people face in life.

MY TAKEAWAY

Some relationships and choices leave a lasting impact on the way we understand love, sacrifice and life.
